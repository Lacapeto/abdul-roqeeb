About

The person behind the circuit diagrams

My name is ALABI ABDULROQEEB, and I am a final-year Computer Engineering student at Ahmadu Bello University (ABU), Zaria. I'm drawn to the point where a physical device meets code, where a sensor reading becomes a decision, and a decision becomes movement, light, or a notification on someone's phone.

Over the course of my studies I've worked across embedded systems, mechatronics, control engineering, robotics and computational intelligence, spending most of my hands-on time with Arduino platforms, Proteus simulation, and IoT development tools. I like projects that force me to think about both sides of a system at once: the firmware running on a microcontroller and the interface a person actually uses.

I entered computer engineering out of a passion for automation, smart connected devices, and the power of intelligent systems to transform human productivity. My goal is to become an innovative systems engineer capable of architecting reliable, autonomous technologies and scalable IoT infrastructure from the circuit board up.

""Build with intent, engineer for impact.""

Outside of coursework, I enjoy a few other things too, head over to the Hobbies page to see what else keeps me busy.
